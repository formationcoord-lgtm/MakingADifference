MAKING A DIFFERENCE IN THE WORLD

9-13 SEPTEMBER

Birkirkara, Malta

What you will learn:

  • The connection between faith, history, and youth leadership
  • How to share and appreciate diverse national faith stories
  • The role of lay people and youth work in the Church and society
  • Practical aspects of Erasmus+ youth exchanges and Youthpass certification
  • Skills for teamwork, communication, and project dissemination

What you will experience:

  • Team-building and cultural exchange with participants from six countries
  • Visits to important faith heritage sites like St Paul’s Grotto and Catacombs
  • Personal reflection time and group discussions on challenges and motivations
  • International evening with food, games, and dancing
  • A closing Mass and a farewell beach event at Golden Bay
The main objective of the training is to empower youth leaders by enhancing their understanding of faith, history, and youth work, while building intercultural connections and practical skills to make a positive impact within their communities and through international youth projects.

Meet the Host Organisation

ŻAK Malta is a well-established organization with extensive experience in Catholic youth work. Known for hosting multiple national and international training programs, it empowers young people through faith-based initiatives that foster spiritual growth, leadership, and active participation in the Church and society.

Participating Organisations

These are the partner organisations: Catholic Action groups from Malta, Italy, Romania and Spain who are full members of FIAC, together with ERKO and Diocese of Limerick. See what they have been up to on their socials.
